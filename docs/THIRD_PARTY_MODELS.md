# Third-party 3D model attribution

The Iron Lab 3D anatomy viewer includes `public/models/ecorche.meshopt.glb` from [Charleeh/ecorche](https://github.com/Charleeh/ecorche), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The model is derived from BodyParts3D (© The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0) and Z-Anatomy by Gauthier Kervyn (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The model is used without geometry changes. Its source includes individually named anatomical structures; the app maps matching muscle names to its existing Iron Lab muscle-training records. The model license applies to the model asset and derivatives, separately from this app's code.

This is an educational visualization and is not for diagnosis or treatment.
